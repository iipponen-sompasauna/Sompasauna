Sompasaunaseura ry

As a member of the Sompasauna Society, you support the activities of Sompasauna.

As a member, you will receive a newsletter and be able to participate in the association's meetings, which of course also include sauna bathing.

The annual fee is optional: 20€, 40€ or 60€, depending on the amount you want to support the association.
