Sompasaunaseura ry

Joining as member of Sompasaunaseura association supports Sompasauna.

As a member, you will receive a newsletter and be able to participate in the association's meetings, which of course also include sauna bathing.

You can pick between the 20€, 40€ or 60€ annual membership fee, depending on the amount you want to support the association.
