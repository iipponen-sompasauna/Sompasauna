SAUNA INFO

1.

Sompasauna is always open

Sompasauna has no staff, you can always come and heat the sauna yourself.

2.

The sauna is free of charge.

If you wish, you can donate money or join the association.

3.

Sompasauna is a mixed sauna for everyone

The sauna does not have separate sauna shifts for women or men.

4.

Live free

You can use the sauna and swim naked or in a swimsuit.

5.

Bring drinking water and a towel.

The sauna does not have running water. There are no showers or washing facilities in the sauna.

6.

Bring your own lock

The sauna does not have changing rooms. There are lockers for valuables, for which you need your own egg lock.

7.

Sompasauna has a zero tolerance policy for sexual harassment.

A person who causes trouble may be banned from the sauna.

8.

No photography or use of speakers is allowed in the sauna.

You can play acoustic music and sing in the sauna, and there is a piano in the sauna.

9.

Don't be a jerk.
