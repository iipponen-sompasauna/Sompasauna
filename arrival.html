Arriving at the sauna

The street address of Sompasauna is

Kansanpuistonpolku 5
00570 Helsinki

You cannot get to the sauna by car, the nearest parking lots are the Mustikkamaa Sports Park parking lot and the parking lot at the beginning of the bridge between Kulosaari and Mustikkamaa.

The Sompasauna is not accessible, but there is a wheelchair ramp for the Temppeli sauna if needed.
